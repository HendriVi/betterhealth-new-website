When to start a conversation

Your experience is the starting point.

The aim is to bring the relevant findings into one understandable picture, then decide which next step is justified.

01

Stress & recovery

You stay switched on, recover slowly or feel that everyday demands have become harder to manage. We look at demand, sleep, routines, bodily state and context.

02

Mental health & persistent symptoms

You want a clearer understanding of ongoing difficulties or an incomplete response to previous support. A clinical assessment may be appropriate.

03

Cognition & performance

Focus, mental stamina or confidence in your performance has changed. The useful question is what explains the change and which factors are modifiable.

04

Long-term brain health

You want to identify relevant risk factors and prioritise prevention. We focus on the actions that fit your history, goals and circumstances.

What a useful plan contains

Clarity you can act on.

The scope is agreed before work begins. Where medical assessment or treatment is indicated, it must be delivered by an appropriately qualified and licensed clinician.

  • A clear summary of the relevant history and current priorities.
  • An explanation of the factors worth investigating and why.
  • A prioritised next step with a defined purpose.
  • A way to judge progress and a point at which to review it.
