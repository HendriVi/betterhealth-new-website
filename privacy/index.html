Privacy

Browsing

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added advertising pixels, third-party analytics, social embeds . GitHub may process technical access information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect the hosting service.

GitHub privacy statement ↗

Forms and the reflection tool

The form and reflection tool run in your browser. Their entries are not submitted to a server by this site and are not saved by our code in cookies or browser storage. Your browser may offer its own autofill features.

Email enquiries

Choosing “Open my email app” passes the prepared text to your email application. You decide whether to send it. Your email provider then handles the message under its own terms. Once you send an enquiry, the recipient receives the details you include so that your request can be answered. Use the first email for general enquiry details and leave out health records.

External links and sharing

External articles and social-sharing destinations have their own privacy practices. Share buttons use your device’s share function or copy a link when you choose to use them. External resources are not embedded or loaded automatically.

Questions about your information

For questions about an enquiry, or to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent, contact hello@betterhealth.ch. Identity, retention and any clinical data arrangements should be clarified with the responsible provider before sensitive information is shared.
