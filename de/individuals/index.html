Who this is for

11

Four situations this is built for

Not everyone needs every assessment, and not everyone needs us.

Mental health with incomplete response

You have a diagnosis and have been treated, and the response has been partial, absent, or bought at a cost in function.

The question we would work on: what is maintaining this, that treatment has not yet addressed?

Cognition and sustained performance

Concentration, cognitive capacity, stress tolerance or recovery are not where they were.

The question we would work on: is this a capacity problem, a state problem, or a demand problem?

Brain health and prevention

You want to understand which factors associated with long-term brain and cognitive health apply to you.

The question we would work on: what is your actual modifiable risk, separated from what is merely measurable?

Complex or unsynthesised cases

Multiple interacting symptoms, several specialists, a stack of reports, and no single model.

The question we would work on: what do these findings look like when read together?

How it works

13

What actually happens, in order

You coordinate nothing.

  1. 01

    Confidential introduction

    What the problem is, what you want changed, and whether this is the right service for it.

  2. 02

    Assessment

    The clinical information and the measurements that are actually justified in your case.

  3. 03

    Interpretation

    Findings are read together and the working model is built.

  4. 04

    Plan

    Interventions, the expected response, the outcomes tracked, and the date of the first review.

  5. 05

    Implementation

    The plan runs, with your existing physicians kept in the loop.

  6. 06

    Reassessment

    The same measures again, compared against what was predicted.

  7. 07

    Adaptation

    Continue what worked, change what did not, and revise the model where the prediction missed.
