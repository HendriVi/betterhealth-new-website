How we decide what to test

06

Testing is useful only when it changes the intervention

Blanket testing produces large volumes of low-value data.

  1. 01

    Question

    What are we trying to clarify clinically?

  2. 02

    Uncertainty

    Which unknown could change the decision?

  3. 03

    Measurement

    What test, marker or observation answers that question?

  4. 04

    Action

    What changes if the result is abnormal, normal or unchanged?

Our rule

If a test cannot plausibly change the decision, it is not part of the roadmap.

From data to decision

07

We do not treat every abnormal value. We build one working model.

Findings are weighed against your symptoms, function, goals, history, current state, risk, constraints and preferences.

  1. 01

    Interpretation

    Each finding is read in the context of the others and of your clinical picture, rather than against a reference range alone.

  2. 02

    Ranked hypotheses

    Competing explanations for what maintains the current state are listed and then ranked. The ranking is the clinical work.

  3. 03

    Prioritised action

    The first step targets the highest-ranked driver that is modifiable and fast to read out, so the model is tested in weeks rather than months.
