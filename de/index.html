Why this is hard

01

Advanced testing is available. Turning those results into a working, tailored plan is rare.

Bloodwork, brain imaging, cognitive testing, physiology, psychology, behaviour and lifestyle are often assessed and treated separately. The individual assessments may be high quality, but what is often missing is the structured process that connects the findings, determines what matters, translates them into a plan, and measures whether that plan worked.

  • 01

    Fragmented information

    Different tests and specialists produce isolated findings. Without a shared model it is difficult to see how the pieces interact or which findings actually matter.

  • 02

    Complex, interacting drivers

    Mental and brain health emerge from interactions between biology, psychology, behaviour and environment. Looking at one system in isolation can miss the mechanisms maintaining the current state.

  • 03

    Weak continuity

    An assessment is only a starting point. What changes over time is rarely compared against what was expected, so plans are continued or abandoned on impression rather than on evidence.
