For organisationsStress & burnout workshop

From explanation to application

Useful on the next working day.

Every session connects a scientific idea with a recognisable work situation, an exercise and a decision participants can take back to their own context.

01

Understand the mechanism

How demands are appraised, how the body prepares to respond, and how sustained pressure can affect recovery and behaviour.

02

Recognise the pattern

Explore overload, uncertainty, attention and recovery through realistic scenarios. Participants choose what to share.

03

Change the conditions

Identify actions at individual, team and leadership level: priorities, workload, boundaries, communication and support.

What your organisation takes away

A shared understanding.
A concrete plan.

Workplace mental health involves the conditions people work in as well as individual skills. Our workshop design reflects that distinction.

Read about burnout at work↗
  • Accessible explanations of key biological and psychological mechanisms.
  • Exercises and scenarios matched to the audience.
  • A practical action sheet for participants and agreed team priorities.
  • A proposed way to review usefulness and progress after the session.
  • A clear distinction between education, managerial action and individual clinical support.
Who are these sessions for?

Employees, leaders, founders and people teams. We adapt the examples and depth to the audience and agree the objectives in advance.

Do you assess employees?

These sessions are educational. Participation does not create a clinical assessment, and employees are not asked to disclose a diagnosis or personal medical information.

What do you need for a proposal?

Your audience, approximate group size, location or online preference, timing and the problem you want the session to address.

What would you like your team to understand and change?

Tell us about your audience and goals. We will shape a relevant workshop proposal.
