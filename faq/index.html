Frequently asked questions

The details that help you decide.

Clear expectations before you take the next step. If your question is not covered, ask us directly.

Individual enquiries

Where do I start?

Send a brief enquiry about your goal. The first exchange clarifies whether the approach fits, the relevant professional, location, scope and fees before you commit.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the agreed scope, assessment and delivery format. You receive the proposed costs before booking. Additional investigations are discussed separately.

Do I need a diagnosis or existing test results?

You can start with a question or concern. Existing reports may help later, but the initial enquiry only needs a brief description of what you want to discuss.

Will I need extensive testing?

Assessment should answer a relevant question. Existing information is reviewed first, and any proposed test should have a clear purpose, explanation of limitations and agreed cost.

Is this covered by insurance?

Coverage depends on the specific service, provider and insurance arrangement. Do not assume reimbursement; confirm the conditions before booking. Corporate workshops are quoted to the organisation.

Can I work with you from outside Switzerland?

Availability depends on the service and location. Educational formats can be discussed for your team; any clinical service requires confirmation of the relevant professional and jurisdiction.

Organisations & learning

Can you tailor a session to our organisation?

Yes. We agree the audience, priorities and learning goals, then shape the examples and exercises around them. Timing, format and fees are set out in the proposal.

Are courses available now?

Corporate workshop enquiries are open. Self-paced courses are in development, with dates and enrolment to be announced once confirmed. The articles and science pages are freely available.

Are workshop participants asked to share medical information?

No. Exercises can be completed using general or fictional examples. Educational sessions do not diagnose participants.

Contact & privacy

How does the enquiry form work?

The form prepares an email on your device. You review it, open your email app and send it yourself. Preparing an email does not send a message to BetterHealth. You can also copy the text into your preferred email service.

What should I include?

Your name, contact address and a brief goal are enough. For a corporate enquiry, include the audience and approximate group size. Keep medical records and sensitive details out of the initial email.
