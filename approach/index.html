The BetterHealth model

02

One process, from what is happening to whether it changed

A diagnosis names a pattern. It leaves open what maintains that pattern in this person, at this point in time. We ask which biological, psychological, behavioural and environmental processes are holding the current state in place, which of them are modifiable, and whether changing them produces the response we predicted.

  1. 01

    Assess

    Clinical picture, physiology, behaviour, context.

  2. 02

    Interpret

    Findings read against each other, not in isolation.

  3. 03

    Prioritise

    Ranked by likely contribution and modifiability.

  4. 04

    Intervene

    The smallest effective step, with a stated expectation.

  5. 05

    Measure

    The outcomes that matter for this person.

  6. 06

    Update

    Compare prediction with outcome, revise the model.

The loop then runs again. Each pass narrows what is still uncertain.

Intervention

09

Intervention follows the formulation, and stays inside medicine

What we do depends on what the assessment found and on what is most likely to move.

  • Psychiatric and psychotherapeutic treatment

    Diagnosis, medication and its optimisation, psychotherapy, risk assessment.

    Established

  • Sleep, activity, nutrition and recovery

    Sleep and circadian intervention, conditioning, nutrition, behavioural change, stress regulation.

    Established Conditional

  • Medical contributors and specialist pathways

    Identified medical contributors are treated through the appropriate medical route.

    Established

  • Selected supplementation

    Only where a deficiency or specific indication has been demonstrated.

    Conditional

  • Neuromodulation and neurofeedback

    Considered in specific presentations. The evidence base varies considerably by method and indication.

    Emerging

  • Coordination

    Holding the model together across everyone involved.

    Conditional

Measuring whether it worked

10

Treatment is not assumed to work because it was delivered

Before anything starts, we state what should change, by roughly when, and how it will be measured.

  1. 01

    Baseline

    Symptoms, function and the relevant measures, recorded before anything changes.

  2. 02

    Intervention

    One clearly defined step, chosen for expected effect and feasibility.

  3. 03

    Expected response

    What should change, in what direction, by roughly when. Stated in advance.

  4. 04

    Reassessment

    The same measures under the same conditions.

  5. 05

    Comparison

    Prediction against outcome. A miss is information, not failure.

  6. 06

    Adjustment

    Continue, modify, or revise the model and rank the hypotheses again.

Outcomes are chosen for the individual.
