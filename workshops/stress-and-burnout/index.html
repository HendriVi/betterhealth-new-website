For organisationsStress & burnout workshop

The 90-minute format

A clear progression from science to action.

An indicative agenda, adapted to your audience. The half-day format adds deeper case work, leadership practice and a more detailed team action plan.

Employees · Leaders · People teams
01

0–10 min · Find the question

Set a shared goal and explore a realistic scenario without personal disclosure.

02

10–30 min · Understand the response

Demand, appraisal, physiological preparation and recovery explained in plain language.

03

30–45 min · Recognise a pattern

Map how uncertainty, routines and working conditions can sustain overload.

04

45–65 min · Practise a change

Work through a team scenario and test a practical adjustment to priorities or communication.

05

65–80 min · Build the next step

Choose one individual action and one organisational action, with an owner and review date.

06

80–90 min · Questions & close

Clarify what participants can try, what needs leadership action and when professional support is appropriate.

Participants leave with

An explanation they can use.

The session is designed around understanding and application. Learning goals and any follow-up are agreed before delivery.
