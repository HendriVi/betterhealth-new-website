About BetterHealth

A better understanding of the person.
A better basis for change.

BetterHealth brings a systems perspective to mental and brain health. We connect the questions about how people feel with the biology, behaviour and environment that shape daily life.

Why we exist

Knowledge should help people make progress.

People often have information without a clear sense of what to do with it. BetterHealth is built around making the picture understandable, identifying the questions that matter and connecting knowledge to practical action.

The same principle guides our work with organisations: explain the mechanisms clearly, relate them to the working environment, then define what can be changed.

01

Clarity

Explain the reasoning in language people can use. Make assumptions and uncertainty visible.

02

Relevance

Choose questions, measurements and actions for their usefulness in the actual situation.

03

Accountability

Define who is responsible, what change is expected and when to review the result.

Clear roles from the first conversation.

Educational sessions explain general principles and practical applications. Clinical assessment, diagnosis and treatment require an appropriately qualified and licensed clinician. The relevant professional, service scope, location and fees must be identified before any clinical engagement.

BetterHealth has a Swiss base and communicates in German and English. Corporate delivery and individual service availability are discussed for your location.
